How the equivalence is computed

Each step of the ladder adds laws; both answers are written in a canonical form with the laws so far. The first step where the two forms are identical is the equivalence found. Laws set to "not accepted" are left out. The last step, equationForms, writes an equation or relation as (left − right) = 0 divided by its leading coefficient, so moving terms between the sides and multiplying both sides by a number give the same form. (In numeric mode the grade comes from the sample points below; the ladder tells which difference was accepted.)
